Solid week — pushed through Days 78–80 of the 100 Days of Code challenge. The SFML stretch is turning into an actual game.

## What I built

Day 78 was SFML audio — sound playback and volume control. Short day but it rounds out the toolkit nicely. Day 79 added score tracking and high score persistence using file I/O. Simple concept but wiring up file reads and writes to keep a high score across sessions felt like a practical callback to earlier file I/O work. Day 80 was the big one — started building Snake. Got the window, grid rendering, a deque-based snake, directional movement, food spawning, and a move timer all wired up. It's still WIP but there's a playable snake moving around the screen eating food.

## What I learned

- Using a `std::deque` for the snake body was a natural fit — push front, pop back, done. One of those moments where picking the right container makes the logic almost trivial.
- The move timer was a good exercise in decoupling game logic from the render loop. The snake moves on its own clock, not every frame, which felt like a step up in how I think about game loops.
- High score persistence is dead simple but it's the kind of feature that makes something feel like a real program instead of a demo.

## Next up

- Finish Snake — collision with walls and self, game over state, restart, and hooking up the score and audio systems from Days 78–79.
- Day 61 lives on, unfilled and unbothered.
