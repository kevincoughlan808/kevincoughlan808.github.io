Lighter week — no commits, no new days logged. Just a breather after wrapping up the 100 Days of Code challenge.

## What happened

Not much, honestly. After pushing through the final stretch and tagging v1.0.0 on the Game of Life project, I didn't write any code this week. Partly intentional, partly inertia. 100 days is a long streak and the week after finishing felt like a natural place to step back.

## Where things stand

Looking back at the full challenge, the trajectory makes sense. Early days were syntax and small programs, middle stretch was SFML and building actual games, final third was patterns, ECS, testing, and profiling. The capstone pulled it all together in a way that felt earned rather than forced. I'm more comfortable with C++ than I was 100 days ago — not expert-level, but confident enough to build things without fighting the language the whole time.

The weak spots are still there though. Multithreading got light coverage. Networking didn't happen at all. And the patterns work was useful but mostly isolated exercises — I want to use them in something real.

## Next up
