Solid week — finished Snake and shifted into design patterns with Days 80–82 of the 100 Days of Code challenge.

## What I built

Day 80 got wrapped up — added collision detection (walls and self), a game over screen with a simple state machine, Enter to restart, and hooked up the score, high score, and sound systems from earlier days. Snake is done and it feels like a complete little game.

Day 81 was design patterns — Singleton Logger and Abstract Factory for spawning different enemy types. Day 82 continued with the Observer pattern using `std::function` callbacks and lambda subscribers. Both days were more about structure than output, but the patterns clicked faster with concrete examples than they ever did from reading about them.

## What I learned

- The state machine for Snake's game over / restart flow was simple (just an enum and a switch) but it made me appreciate how quickly even a small game needs one. Without it the logic was getting tangled.
- Singleton Logger felt almost too easy, which I think is the point — and also why people warn about overusing it. Abstract Factory was more interesting because it forced me to think about where object creation belongs.
- Observer with lambdas felt very natural in C++. Registering callbacks with `std::function` is clean and it's easy to see how this scales to event systems in games.
