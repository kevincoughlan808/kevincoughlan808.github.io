## What happened

Third week with no commits. At this point I can't really call it a cooldown anymore — it's just not coding. The "pick a concrete project by next week" plan from last post didn't happen, which is annoying but not surprising. Telling yourself to decide isn't the same as deciding.

The raw sockets idea still makes sense. I've done some reading on POSIX sockets and the basic flow isn't complicated — socket, bind, listen, accept, read, write. The conceptual overhead is lower than I expected. The problem isn't that it's hard, it's that I keep researching instead of writing code. At some point you have to stop reading man pages and just open a file.

## What's actually in the way

Honestly, I think the 100 Days structure was doing more work than I realized. Having a numbered day and a clear "do one thing and commit" constraint made it easy to start. Without that, every session turns into "what should I build" instead of "build the next thing." I need to recreate some version of that structure, even if it's not another 100-day challenge.
