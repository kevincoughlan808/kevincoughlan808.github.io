Solid week — pushed through Days 71–74 of the 100 Days of Code challenge. Shifted gears into graphics with SFML.

## What I built

Day 71 was SFML setup — window creation, setting the framerate, and handling the close event. Feels like the "hello world" of graphics programming but there's something satisfying about getting that first window to pop up. Day 72 moved into drawing primitives — circles, rectangles, and convex shapes. Simple stuff but good for building intuition about the render loop.

Day 73 was textures and sprites — loaded a PNG, scaled it, rotated it, positioned it on screen. Day 74 added input handling — keyboard movement and mouse click position. By the end of the day I had a sprite I could move around with arrow keys, which felt like a big jump from printing to stdout.

## What I learned

- SFML's API is refreshingly clean. After weeks of low-level performance work and threading, something like `window.draw(sprite)` feels almost too easy.
- The render loop pattern — clear, draw, display — is simple but it's the kind of thing that clicks better by writing it than by reading about it.
- Input handling made me think about frame-rate independence for the first time. Moving a sprite by a fixed offset per frame works fine at a locked framerate but I can already see why delta time matters.
